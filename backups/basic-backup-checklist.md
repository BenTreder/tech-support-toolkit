- Documents
- Photos
- Business files
- Password backups

## Backup Locations

- External drives
- Cloud backups
- Secondary devices

Personal use:

```txt
Weekly
```

Business use:

```txt
Daily
```

Critical systems:

```txt
Multiple times per day
```
