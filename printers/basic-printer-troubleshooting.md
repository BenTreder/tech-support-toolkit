# Basic Printer Troubleshooting

## Check Basics

- Power connection
- Paper
- Ink/Toner
- Error lights

---

## Restart Devices

Restart:

- Printer
- Computer
- Network router (if wireless printer)

---

## Driver Issues

- Reinstall printer drivers
- Update drivers

---

## Wireless Issues

Reconnect printer to Wi-Fi if needed.

---

## Clear Print Queue

Remove stuck print jobs.
